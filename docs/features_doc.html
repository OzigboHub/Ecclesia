1. System Architecture & High-Level Overview

Ecclesia is an enterprise-grade Catholic parish management platform and community portal supporting multi-tenant hierarchy (Parish & Outstation), fine-grained Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), modular feature toggling, step-up authentication, sacramental registry records, Paystack-backed financial stewardship, automated liturgical mass schedules, pastoral appointments, and rich public timelines.

Key Architectural Pillars:
  • Multi-Tenant Hierarchy: Supports top-level Parishes and child Outstations with full data segregation and aggregated deanery/diocesan reporting.
  • Step-Up Security Ladder: Multi-factor authentication (TOTP & Passkeys), 24h session policies, device tracking, and sensitive-action re-authentication gates.
  • Modular Feature Toggle Engine: 18+ granular toggles per organization allowing custom operational setups.
  • Integrated Financial Pipeline: Paystack wallet architecture with automated subaccounts, Dedicated Virtual Accounts (DVA), split payouts, and cash ledgering.

2. Core Functional Modules Breakdown

2.1 Multi-Tenant Organization & Hierarchy

  • Two-Level Scoping: Parent Parish to child Outstations via relational parentId.
  • Session Boundary: All DB operations are strictly scoped to session.user.organizationId.
  • Inheritance & Roll-ups: Parish Admins oversee all child outstations; Outstation Admins are restricted to their local scope.

2.2 Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) & Security Ladder

  • 8 System Roles: SUPER_ADMIN, DIOCESE_ADMIN, DEANERY_ADMIN, PARISH_ADMIN, PARISH_SECRETARY, PARISH_STAFF, SOCIETY_PRESIDENT, and PARISHIONER.
  • TOTP 2FA & Backup Codes: QR-code enrollment with encrypted backup keys and instant lockout protection.
  • WebAuthn / Passkeys: Biometric login support for modern mobile and desktop browsers.
  • Step-Up Re-Auth Gates: Critical actions (e.g. updating bank payout accounts) demand password/passkey re-verification.
  • Parish Gate Codes: Optional 4-6 digit PIN gate restricting public timelines for private parishes.

2.3 Parishioner Registry & Sacramental Logbooks

  • Parishioner Directory: Contact information, residential address, occupational tags, outstation, and family groupings.
  • Batch CSV Import: Bulk onboarding engine with schema validation, phone normalization (E.164), and duplicate detection.
  • Sacramental Records: Full lifecycle tracking for Baptism, First Holy Communion, Confirmation, Holy Matrimony, and Holy Orders.
  • Certificate Generation: Automated PDF sacramental certificates with official parish headers and verification folios.

2.4 Liturgical Mass Management & Mass Intentions

  • Schedule Templates: Recurring mass templates (e.g., Weekday 6:30 AM, Sunday 8:00 AM / 10:30 AM).
  • Auto-Generation Engine: Automated creation of daily/Sunday mass instances up to 90 days in advance.
  • Mass Intention Booking: Parishioner online/offline booking with intention types (Thanksgiving, Repose of Soul, Special Intentions).
  • Stipend Accounting & Quotas: Paystack online payment or cash marking, with strict per-mass intention caps to prevent overcrowding.
  • Priest Mass Sheet Export: Instant PDF/print layout of booked intentions for the altar.

2.5 Financial Management & Paystack Wallet Integration

  • Ledger Categories: Tithes, Sunday Collections, Thanksgiving, Harvest Levies, Project Funds, Society Dues, Mass Stipends.
  • Paystack Subaccount & DVA: Automatic transaction splitting, Dedicated Virtual Bank Accounts per parish, instant receipts.
  • Cash Entry & Count Sheets: Multi-counter physical count logging with envelope tracking.
  • Withdrawal Approvals: Secure multi-step withdrawal workflow to disburse parish wallet funds to verified bank accounts.
  • Financial Analytics: Interactive revenue charts, monthly breakdown tables, and exportable financial audits.

2.6 Pastoral Appointments & Confessions

  • Priest Availability: Configurable recurring availability hours and custom blocked/blackout dates.
  • Booking Lifecycle: PENDING → APPROVED / REJECTED → COMPLETED / CANCELLED.
  • Real-time Alerts: Instant push and email notifications on booking updates.

2.7 Societies & Pious Associations

  • Society Directory: Sacred Heart, CWO, CMO, CYON, Legion of Mary, St. Vincent de Paul, Choir, etc.
  • Executive Governance: Role assignments for President, Secretary, and Financial Secretary.
  • Dues & Levies Tracking: Automated monthly dues calculation, payment logging, and arrears reporting.

2.8 Public Parish Feed & Mobile Giving (/feed, /give, /me)

  • Live Parish Timeline: Pinned notices, daily saint reflections, upcoming liturgical events.
  • Quick Giving (/give): Fast, mobile-optimized giving portal via Card, Bank Transfer, or USSD.
  • Member Portal (/me): Giving history, booked mass intentions, appointments, and active device sessions.
